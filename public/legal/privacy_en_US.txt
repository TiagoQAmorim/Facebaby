FaceBaby — Privacy Policy

EN-US - Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction
Welcome to FaceBaby.
FaceBaby is an application intended to record, monitor and organize information related to child
development, including feeding, sleep, diaper changes, growth, developmental milestones, memories,
photographs, reports and Artificial Intelligence-assisted features.
This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share and protect user data. By using FaceBaby,
you agree to this Privacy Policy.
2. Data Collected
We may collect the following data:
Account Data
• Name
• Email address
• Account identifier
• Profile photo, when provided
• Authentication method used
Baby Data
• Baby name
• Date of birth
• Sex
• Weight
• Height
• Development information
• Feeding records
• Sleep records
• Diaper change records
• Important milestones
• Information voluntarily entered by parents or guardians
Photographs and Submitted Content
• Photographs
• Descriptions
• Captions
• Comments
• Registered memories
Technical Data
• Device model
• Operating system
• App version
• Error logs
• Diagnostic information
• IP address
• Performance data
Subscription Data
When applicable, we may process subscription status, plan type and renewal date. FaceBaby does not
store full credit card details. Payments are processed by Google Play, Apple App Store or authorized

providers.
3. Use of Information
The collected data may be used for app operation, cloud synchronization, information backup, report
generation, creation of memory books, experience personalization, notification delivery, user support,
product improvements, platform security, fraud prevention and compliance with legal obligations.
4. Artificial Intelligence
FaceBaby may provide Artificial Intelligence features to assist users. These features may analyze feeding
records, sleep records, growth records, registered events and information provided by the user.
Responses generated by Artificial Intelligence are for informational purposes only, do not constitute
medical advice, do not replace medical consultations, do not replace professional diagnosis and should
not be used as the sole basis for decisions related to health. Always consult qualified professionals for
medical matters.
5. Public Content Sharing
Some features may allow users to voluntarily share public content, such as Photo of the Week, public
memories and community highlights. By making content public, the user agrees that it may be viewed by
other FaceBaby users. The user may remove or change the visibility of such content when the feature is
available.
6. Content Moderation
FaceBaby reserves the right to remove inappropriate content, restrict posts, suspend accounts or
terminate accounts that violate the Terms of Use. Illegal, offensive, discriminatory, violent, pornographic
content or content that exposes third parties without authorization is prohibited.
7. Data Storage
Data may be stored in third-party services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Firebase
Storage and Google Cloud Platform. Servers may be located in different countries. By using FaceBaby,
the user agrees to the international transfer of data necessary for the operation of the service.
8. Security
We adopt reasonable technical and administrative measures to protect user information, including
secure authentication, encryption in transit, access control, security monitoring and backups. No system
is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
9. Children and Minors
FaceBaby is intended for parents, legal guardians and caregivers. Data entered about children is
provided by guardians. FaceBaby does not intentionally collect data directly from children without the
participation of their legal guardians.
10. User Rights
The user may request access to their data, correction of information, account deletion, deletion of stored
data, export of information and revocation of applicable consents. Requests may be made through the
official support channels.
11. Account Deletion
The user may request account deletion. After deletion, data may be permanently removed, some
information may be retained when required by law and backups may remain for a limited period.
12. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated periodically. When relevant changes are made, users may be notified
through the app or other appropriate channels. Continued use of FaceBaby after changes constitutes
acceptance of the new version of the Policy.
13. Contact
For questions related to privacy or data protection, contact: suporte@facebaby.app, or use the official
channels made available in the app.
14. Legal Compliance
This Policy seeks to comply with applicable data protection laws, including the Brazilian General Data
Protection Law (LGPD), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - European Union) and other
applicable laws according to the user region. In the event of conflict between laws, the law mandatory for
the applicable jurisdiction shall prevail.
