Features

Designed for busy parents who want clarity, connection, and peace of mind.

AI Nanny

Ask questions, log events by voice or text, and get helpful summaries powered by artificial intelligence.

Baby Records

Keep feeding, sleep, diapers, medicines, and daily notes organized in one timeline.

Growth Tracking

Monitor weight, height, and development with visual growth curves and history.

Sleep Tracking

Log naps and nighttime sleep to understand patterns and share updates with family.

Feeding Tracking

Record breastfeeding, bottles, and solids with quick taps or voice commands.

Vaccination Records

Store vaccine history and upcoming immunization reminders in one secure place.

Family Sharing
